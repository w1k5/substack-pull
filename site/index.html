GETTING STARTED

Open Clearview and connect

  1. On Mac, open the disk image and drag Clearview into Applications. For the Windows ZIP, extract the whole folder and open Clearview.
  2. Choose Sign in to Substack. If you manage several publications, pick one from the list.
  3. Open the dashboard to explore your posts, Notes, and subscriber sources.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your data stays on your device

Clearview connects directly to Substack and stores your session in your operating system’s credential store. Changes to Substack can interrupt syncing.

To use Claude Desktop or Codex, open Clearview → AI Connections after signing in. Both clients can start Clearview’s bundled connection locally.

If macOS asks you to verify Clearview

Apple hasn’t notarized this beta, so macOS may say it can’t verify the app. Only continue if you trust the download from this release.

  1. Move Clearview into Applications and try opening it from there.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to Security.
  3. Click Open Anyway for Clearview and confirm when prompted.
