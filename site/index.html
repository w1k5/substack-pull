GETTING STARTED

Open Clearview and connect

  1. On Mac, open the disk image and drag Clearview into Applications. For the Windows ZIP, extract the whole folder and open Clearview.
  2. Choose Connect Substack and sign in.
  3. Open the dashboard to explore your posts, Notes, and subscriber sources.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your data stays on your device

Clearview connects directly to Substack and stores your session in your operating system’s credential store. Changes to Substack can interrupt syncing.

Compatible AI clients can optionally connect to the local MCP server. Clearview works on its own.
