How to read this chart

Horizontal position compares reach with your usual Note at the same age. Vertical position shows the selected action per 1,000 impressions, so a widely seen Note does not automatically look more compelling. Likes, replies (comments on Notes), restacks, and shares remain separate. Reader actions are lifetime totals; reach pace is age matched. Rates require at least 100 impressions; audience share requires at least 100 people. No single measure defines success, and these patterns do not establish cause.
