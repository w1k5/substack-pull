How to read this chart

Horizontal position compares reach with your usual Note at the same age. Responses combine likes, replies, shares, and restacks. Reader actions and audience are lifetime totals. Rates require at least 100 impressions; audience share requires at least 100 people. These patterns suggest what to investigate, not what caused them.
